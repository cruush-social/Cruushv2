# Why I built cruush and want people to own their technology

I’m Julien, a queer trans man and the founder and lead developer of cruush. I built it because I was tired of opening cruising apps hoping for connection and receiving rape threats instead. I wanted control over who could see me and reach me, and a place where queer people across genders and sexualities could find connection, pleasure, and community.

cruush should be worth returning to because of its features and its people. I want queer sex to feel fun, magical, and dignified, with discretion and visibility both available by choice. Queer parties at BASEMENT showed me that this kind of shared community can work. cruush’s camp, lore, and parody of satanic ritual panic bring that spirit online.

## An independent, family-funded project

I run [technosovereign](https://technosovereign.com/), an independent creative technology studio in New York. In nine months, working roughly 60 hours a week, I built three original products with about $10,000 from my family buying equity. There is no venture capital, and that budget does not include a salary for my time.

I lead the code, architecture, branding, and visual design. I commissioned our custom logo and icons and drew some visuals in Figma. My cofounder is currently focused on school and AI research; a cybersecurity expert has also supported the app through audits. I’m the only developer currently building cruush full time, and the person responding when something breaks.

Working for myself gives my autistic focus, imagination, and persistence somewhere to go. I make software I want to exist but cannot find.

## What I’ve built

- **[cruush](https://technosovereign.com/work/cruush):** a queer social and cruising app with custom messaging, location privacy, automated moderation, and payment infrastructure.
- **[sovereignpay](https://technosovereign.com/work/sovereignpay):** the cryptocurrency processor I built for cruush, with payment links, invoices, transaction tracking, and payments into merchants’ own wallets.
- **[HEKA](https://technosovereign.com/work/hekatonomous):** an agent product for local models or your own API tokens, with persistent tasks, computer control, voice, and remote interfaces.

HEKA and sovereignpay’s consumer releases are next. I also want to develop an open-source accessibility agent from HEKA’s codebase with blind users.

## Privacy and ownership

**What happens in cruush, stays on cruush.** I will never sell cruush, sell your data, or use it to train AI. Sensitive content is encrypted on the server; authorized server access remains possible for operation and moderation. End-to-end encryption is still on the development list.

I don’t want dark patterns, harmful “taboo” promotions, or open drug markets turning our vulnerability into revenue. I want people to own their technology and our community to benefit from what it creates.

I self-host with European-owned Hetzner; the current server is in Virginia. European infrastructure, independent payment processing, and eventually an off-grid version are goals I’m working toward.

## What’s next

The next development priorities include end-to-end encrypted messaging and a global IRC chat.

I also want Labyrinths to become cruush’s defining feature: branching encounters where two people shape one story through their choices. Players set private boundaries and can leave at any time. The prototype demonstrates branching text and turn progression; pairing and delivery are simulated. Personalized stories and additional modes remain in development.
