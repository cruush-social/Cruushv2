# cruush changelog

## Release 2.4 (October 13, 2026)

- Recently active Ghost members now show Online. Dark Room members show Online on profiles you already have permission to view, with location privacy preserved.
- Expanded map pins open the selected profile on the first deliberate tap.
- Full-profile Vibes shrink onto one line, and the labels Queer arts and Queer nightlife now read Arts and Nightlife.
- Long Health capsule text wraps inside its card, and full-profile Signals fit all 35 characters on one line.
- The Purgatory card stays readable and scrollable while the keyboard is open.
- Incoming cruush profiles and Back controls open the correct destination.
- View-once message photos open in a full-screen viewer, and conversation presence, scrolling and composer clearance are more reliable.
- Confessional preserves the Never time filter, and Carousel photos refresh when photo permissions change.
- Login, installation, notification and reward prompts queue more consistently.
- cruush+ profiles can select up to 10 kinks; Basic profiles retain 3.
- Written survey answers are optional; ratings and dropdown answers remain required.
- New checkout offers PayPal PYUSD on Arbitrum, Venmo PYUSD on Ethereum, and wallet USDC on Base. Cash App and Bitcoin are no longer offered for new orders; historical orders remain available.
- Payment instructions match the selected method, with a beginner stablecoin guide and payment support after 30 minutes without confirmation.
- Ghost Mode location protection is stronger, with tighter database access controls and verified backup restoration.

## Release 2.3 (October 11, 2026)

- Conversations open at the newest loaded message, while scrolling back keeps older messages accessible.
- Popup Mode opens pages and Coven chats in the existing popups.
- Members can leave Covens and group conversations from their chat controls.
- Long relationship-status text wraps within its profile card instead of overlapping Looking For.
- View-once message photos remain available to retry when storage or decryption fails before delivery.
- The sale banner is more translucent, and compact mobile filter notices wrap more clearly.

## Release 2.2 (October 10, 2026)

- Selected profile options fit up to two capsules per row when space allows, including boi and butch in Presentation.
- DL Mode now masks explicit profile photos in the Edit Profile badge-picker heart preview.
- Last active reflects app use rather than the last location update. Recently active members show Online; Ghost Mode members do not.
- Profile refreshes keep newer cruush and membership state when responses arrive out of order.
- Purgatory wording clarifies that the service area applies to the Sinner’s Map. Its status glyph has no surrounding border, and its bottom Map button opens Purgatory.
- Onboarding and Purgatory use the new glossy animated emblem, with the old overlay removed.
- Trash controls sit at the bottom-right of incoming-cruush and profile-view cards on the Cruushes page.
- Mobile profile popups have the updated header controls and Close button, and Map Controls sit closer to the top bar.
- Checkout funding explanations now sit inside the existing collapsible payment instructions.

## Release 2.1 (October 10, 2026)

- Long message previews stay inside their cards, and Vibe capsules fit on one line.
- Chat has less space between the message composer and bottom navigation, with a shorter lower fade.
- Confessional shows newest posts first, defaults to the past 30 days and Global distance, and keeps loading while matching posts remain.
- Nearby and expanded map pins clear your own pin at close zoom; Spot clusters stay fixed while panning at the same zoom.
- Mile High Club profiles use their selected location, and the Purgatory screen fits better on mobile.
- cruush+ check-ins can target Crowds, with optional Pink Book messages following the selected audience.
- Coven settings now let owners choose whether joining requires approval.
- Mobile checkout QR codes and protected-order explanations can collapse while the countdown and payment controls stay available.
- Unavailable controls use the approved dimmed gradient, and notification banners fit the window more consistently.
- Banned accounts and their content are excluded from member-facing views; connected open screens refresh to remove them.

## Release 2.0 (October 9, 2026)

- Use cruush from anywhere; the map remains limited to supported locations.
- The updated UI brings clearer controls and smoother navigation across the map, profiles, Feed, Messages, and Clubs.
- Follow Bitcoin orders for cruush+ and Pulse with clearer payment and confirmation progress.
- Choose more Feed post durations and control mutual Feed-post notifications.
- Bug fixes and stability improvements throughout the app.

## Release 1.9 (October 5, 2026)

- Browse persistent Clubs from Messages with separate My Clubs and Browse Clubs views.
- cruush+ members can create a Club with a mutual cruush, choose its visibility, and add searchable tags.
- Each Club has a linked chat, member roles, moderation controls, sharing, and join-request review.
- Club membership records and private application answers now use encrypted storage.
- The Badge picker adds 111 Kink icons and 333 Vibe icons, searchable by name or category.
- Side-badge choices and short titles stay with your profile and appear on map hearts.

## Release 1.8 (September 25, 2026)

- Pay for cruush+ or Pulse with Bitcoin from Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, or another compatible wallet.
- Bitcoin checkout shows the exact amount and address, a QR code, copy controls, and a 24-hour payment window.
- Track payment progress through six confirmations and return to an unfinished checkout without losing its details.
- Order history in Settings keeps cruush+ and Pulse purchases visible, including confirmation and activation status.
- Fixed-term cruush+ purchases never auto-renew. New reminders arrive three days and one day before access ends.
- Billing support now has dedicated choices for payment issues, subscriptions or renewals, Pulse purchases, and refund requests.
- Feed posts can now reach more than one Crowd at a time.
- Mobile onboarding stays readable and scrollable around the keyboard, including long option lists.
- Map gestures and recentering are steadier: pinching stays on the map instead of zooming the page.
- Sign-in retries and photo uploads recover more reliably when a connection drops.
- Badge frames now keep Pride symbols inside their borders, with clearer sizing across the map and profiles.

## Release 1.7 (September 15, 2026)

- DL Mode now shows a lit state on the map, and controls which photo audiences see masked covers.
- Badges expand with collar emblems, Pride symbols, frames, hanky code, and the twelve zodiac signs.
- Relationship status is now a Seeking filter and shows on your profile.
- Read receipts show when your direct message was read.
- Notifications add Do Not Disturb hours.
- Color vision (color-blind) accessibility modes and larger text options are available across the app.
- Push notifications now prompt automatically after install and after onboarding.
- The Spot directory is cleaner: duplicate, unnamed, and mislabeled Spots removed, transit stops added, categories reorganized.
- Sign the Code of Conduct from Settings.
- Public report and suspension appeal forms, with a dedicated sign-in screen for suspended accounts.

## Release 1.6 (September 1, 2026)

- Explore from another location with Mile High Club.
- Find Spots by vibe and amenities, see who is there, and read their Pink Book while checked in.
- Browse a larger Spot directory with new Adult shop, Music venue, and Sports venue categories.
- Spot check-ins expire. Pink Book posts stay until their author deletes them.
- Post text to Feed for free, search #tags, and add up to three photos with cruush+.
- Complete Side Quests to earn bonus Pulses and cruush+ days.
- Ghost Mode keeps its familiar animation with the new 1.6 icon, while Dark Room adds a private way to browse.
- Profiles, messages, map controls, loading states, and mobile layouts feel faster and clearer.

## Release 1.5 (August 20, 2026)

- Cruise browsing now shows full 4:5 profile photos with profile details and actions layered in dark glass controls.
- Your Seeking filters now stay selected while you browse profiles.
- Activity tabs now use more consistent layouts, cruush actions, and top-three Vibe labels.
- Nearby alerts now take you directly to the relevant person’s map pin while respecting their location-sharing settings.
- Mobile sign-up now scrolls reliably, with themed checkboxes and clearer footer spacing.
- Map controls and top actions stay separated on small screens.
- Survey responses are delivered more reliably so feedback reaches the cruush team.
