new in v1.6

Spots are here

Filter by category and vibe, see Who's Here before checking in, and share vibes, amenities, or access tips. Check-ins expire. Pink Book posts stay until their author deletes them. Spot clusters and people clusters stay separate on the map.

 

Explore farther

Mile High Club lets cruush+ members drop a travel pin anywhere in the current map area. Search an address or enter GPS coordinates, then drop your pin.
 

More from Feed

The Feed is open to everyone. Basic members can post text and add up to three profile tags. cruush+ adds up to 10 profile tags and up to three photo hearts.
 

Badges, your way

Choose the Badges beside your heart and what each side says. Badge borders follow your heart color, including Mile High Club sky.
 

More polish everywhere

Complete Side Quests for rewards, use the new Ghost Mode icon with its familiar animation, and move through clearer profiles and messages.
