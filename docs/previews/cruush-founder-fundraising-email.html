Ownership, independence, and giving back

My privacy principle: What happens in cruush, stays on cruush. I’ll never sell cruush, even for a trillion dollars, or sell your data or train AI on it. I alone administer our Virginia server and database with European-owned Hetzner; access is protected through my phone and fingerprint. I work for myself; no employer can fire me from this work, and I won’t let a board push me out of cruush. I’m the only person I trust with this server. They’ll have to rip this key from my cold, dead hands. Sensitive content is encrypted server-side; authorized moderation/operational access remains. E2EE is future work.

I plan to offer cruush.eu as an alternative server option on European infrastructure and eventually build my own server in Europe, aiming for total sovereignty and control. European card processing is also planned. If you’re a European citizen interested in a partnership, contact julien@cruush.app. I need help setting up the European side of the business and arranging card payments. I’ll require ID checks and related access restrictions only where legally required. When that day comes, I’ll build verification in-house and use it to offer optional verified badges. I want less dependence on American services and will explore alternatives to American bank underwriting that let cruush preserve its lawful adult content. My final goal: an off-grid version using compatible powered Meshtastic nodes, without a central server/company, outlasting me. A spark in the dark. Later ideas: spot navigation and area-only chat designed to disappear when everyone leaves.

Future goals: profit-sharing for every member, queer technology/nightlife grants, and trans/GNC chemsex residential recovery and a halfway house in NYC. I’m open to nonprofit status; I believe a sustainable LLC could give back more.
