new in v1.8

Bitcoin checkout is here

Pay for fixed-term cruush+ or one-time Pulse packs with Bitcoin from Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, or another compatible wallet.

 

Exact details, easy to follow

Your checkout shows the exact Bitcoin amount and address, a QR code, copy controls, and a 24-hour payment window.
 

Track every order

Follow payment progress through six confirmations. Return to an unfinished checkout without losing its details, or find cruush+ and Pulse purchases later in Settings under Order history.
 

No surprise renewals

Fixed-term cruush+ purchases never auto-renew. We will remind you three days and one day before access ends.
 

More than checkout

  • Share one Feed post with more than one Crowd.
  • Move through mobile onboarding with clearer prompts and option lists around the keyboard.
  • Pinch and recenter the map without zooming the whole page.
  • Get more reliable sign-in retries and photo uploads after connection drops.
  • See Pride symbols stay neatly inside their badge frames.
 

Billing support, when you need it

Support now has dedicated choices for payment issues, subscriptions or renewals, Pulse purchases, and refund requests.
