cruush update

This update tightens message privacy, improves discovery, and makes your feed and privacy controls clearer.

Open settings

Messages default to Mutuals

New accounts default to Mutuals, so only mutual cruushes can start chats unless you choose Public or Crowd in Settings.

Better message discovery

The message picker now separates Public and Mutuals, adds sorting, and shows more context like pronouns, age, distance, shared crowd info, and visible feed pins.

 

More feed control

Feed settings now include a 7-day post window, a broader nearby radius, and clearer controls for when your feed map pin appears.

 

Clearer privacy audit

Privacy Audit now summarizes where you are visible, who can message you, and one-to-one precise-location access you can revoke.
