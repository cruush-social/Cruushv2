DL Mode, Badges, and more

v1.7 brings a clearer DL Mode, a bigger Badge set, and better notifications and accessibility.

See what changed

new in v1.7

DL Mode is clearer

DL Mode now shows a lit state on the map, and you control which photo audiences see masked covers.

 

Badges expand

Collar emblems, Pride symbols, frames, hanky code, and the twelve zodiac signs join the Badge menu.
 

Know more, control more

Relationship status is now a Seeking filter and shows on your profile. Direct messages show read receipts. Notifications add Do Not Disturb hours.
 

Easier to read, easier to find

Color vision accessibility modes and larger text options are available across the app. Push notifications now prompt automatically after install and after onboarding.
 

A cleaner Spot directory

Duplicate, unnamed, and mislabeled Spots are removed, transit stops are added, and categories are reorganized.
 

Safety and trust

Sign the Code of Conduct from Settings. Public report and suspension appeal forms now have a dedicated sign-in screen for suspended accounts.
