Crypto first. On our terms.

Written by Julien Lenaz, admin and lead developer of cruush. September 12, 2026

Crypto was always going to be a way to pay for cruush. We’re moving it up because more than 20 American payment processors gave us the same answer: remove all NSFW content, including nudity in private messages between adults, before we’ll process your payments.

That’s a ridiculous ask for a queer cruising app.

A legal business can still be turned down for card processing. The processors we approached also had to satisfy their banking partners, which underwrite payments and take on financial risk. After high-profile trafficking cases and lawsuits involving banks, our view is that fear of liability helps drive blanket restrictions on adult platforms. Consensual adult content gets swept into the same rejection, even when a business actively screens for abuse.

We take child abuse and human trafficking extremely seriously. We use some of the most advanced screening software in the industry, from the same provider used by Feeld, to screen all user-submitted content across cruush, including photos, messages, posts and profiles, for CSAM, bestiality, nonconsensual imagery, hate symbols, gore and spam. We’re not aware of another cruising service screening all user-submitted content this way. Our custom-built automod acts on the tags returned by our screening providers. Photos use encrypted connections and short-lived access links for screening. Our providers don’t retain content submitted for screening, and our agreements prohibit using members’ content to train AI models.

Keeping abuse out matters. Consenting adults sharing nudes belongs here.

Our crypto checkout will let you buy cruush+ and extra Pulses through Cash App, PayPal, Venmo or a compatible wallet. We support dollar-pegged stablecoins and Bitcoin, including Bitcoin for Cash App users in New York where its stablecoin option isn’t available. No extra checkout account or new app required if you already have a supported, crypto-enabled account.

Each order gets a unique receiving address linked to your account, an exact amount and a QR code. Bitcoin’s quoted amount stays fixed for that order. You have 24 hours for payment to arrive on-chain; checkout tracks receipt and Bitcoin’s progress through six confirmations before activation. Confirmations can finish after that deadline. Switching payment methods before sending creates a fresh order and address.

Checkout shows exactly what to send, including our collection fee, plus a suggested balance with extra room for your app’s sending fee. Unused crypto stays yours. Other wallets calculate their own sending fees.

Crypto subscriptions are one-time purchases. No auto-renewal. When yours runs out, you choose whether to buy another.

And your bank statement won’t have a cruush merchant charge from that crypto payment. Buying the crypto can show the app or provider you used. Your cruising subscription stays out of that statement.

We’re applying to European card processors that work with legal adult businesses. If that means incorporating in Europe or opening a European bank account, we’re willing to do it. We want to build a European home for cruush, at cruush.eu or something similar, with the same worldwide map. Our goal: no ID checks to browse unless we’re legally required to introduce them. Crypto stays when cards arrive.

From where we’re standing, established platforms have a head start and the kind of pull a new cruising app doesn’t. We call it “fuck you money”: making so much money that businesses look the other way about their terms. We don’t have that yet. Our ambition is the full, uncensored cruush in the official stores too.

For now, we’re planning censored iOS and Android store versions, full Android downloads directly from cruush, and full versions through alternative stores that allow NSFW content, including iOS where those stores are available. Same account, people, profiles, messages and membership across versions and the web.

More ways to get on cruush. More ways to support it. Your choice.
