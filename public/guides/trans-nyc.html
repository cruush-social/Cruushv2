Meet trans people in NYC

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Describing yourself

You choose how your profile reads. The gender list includes trans woman, trans man, transmasc, transfemme, nonbinary, genderfluid and two-spirit, along with many others. Pronouns are separate, and the list includes she/her, he/him, they/them, xe/xem, ze/zir and fae/faer. There is also an option that says to ask.

Photos you control

You can have up to four photos on your profile, and none of them needs to show your face. Only signed-in people you have allowed can see them. If you block someone, their access to your photos ends immediately.

Where you appear

Your exact position is not shown by default. People see you somewhere inside an area. If you want people to find you more precisely, you can share precise location with mutual cruushes only, and you can turn on Ghost Mode if you would rather be shown at a decoy position.

When someone crosses a line

Block them from their profile and they are gone from your map at once. Reporting is right beside it, and a person reads each report.

Saying what you want

Clear messages save time for everyone. If you are looking for sex, say so. If you want to talk first, say that. If someone cannot respect it, you have learned something before meeting them instead of after.

Getting around

New York's subway runs all night and covers most of the city, so you can pick a meeting place near where you live or somewhere you already feel comfortable. Choosing the place is your right, and it is a reasonable way to see how someone responds.

Taking your time

You can look at the map without sending anything. You do not have to cruush back anyone who cruushes you.

Coverage

cruush works in and around New York City, within about 100 miles of it.
