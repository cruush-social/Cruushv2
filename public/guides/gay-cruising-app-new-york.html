A gay cruising app for New York City

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Which labels can you pick?

The sexuality list includes gay, achillean, bisexual and queer. Gender options include man, cis man and trans man. Body type labels include bear, otter, cub, twink, muscular and lean, and presentation labels include masc. All of them are optional.

How does the map work?

Open cruush and you see who is nearby, who is passing through and who is up for meeting now. If you want someone to know you noticed them, send a cruush. If they send one back, you are mutual.

What photos can you use?

Up to four photos per profile. A face is not required, and only signed-in people you have allowed can see them.

Where do people meet in New York?

New York has queer nightlife and meeting places across all five boroughs. cruush does not list venues. It shows people, and you can move your pin to a neighborhood to see who is around before you head over.

Can you use it while traveling through?

If you are in New York for a night, passing through is one of the three groups on the map, so people can see you are not local. It saves explaining and sets expectations.

Where does cruush work?

cruush works in and around New York City, within about 100 miles of it.
