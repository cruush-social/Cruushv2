A cruising app for women

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

What cruising means here

Cruising means looking for someone to meet, often soon. On cruush that starts with a map of who is nearby, and it is built for queer people of every gender, so women use it alongside everyone else.

How to start

  1. Pick the labels that fit you from the gender and sexuality lists, then add pronouns if you want to.
  2. Add up to four photos. None of them needs to show your face.
  3. Open the map and look around before you do anything.
  4. Send a cruush to someone you are interested in.
  5. Talk first. If you decide to meet, choose a public place.

Keeping control

A cruush is only interest, not an invitation to everything. When it becomes mutual, you decide which parts of your profile open up. Blocking someone is on their profile and works immediately.

Why people use it

Some women want someone nearby tonight. Others want to meet a few people before deciding anything. A map helps with both, because you can see who is actually around and how close, rather than who might be in the same city.

What a good first message looks like

Short and specific works best. Say something about their profile, say what you are looking for, and ask a question. Nobody has to answer, and you do not have to keep chatting if the replies feel off.

Coverage

cruush works in and around New York City, within about 100 miles of it.
