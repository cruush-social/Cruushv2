Bisexual cruising

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Labels

The sexuality list includes bisexual, pansexual, queer, omnisexual, polysexual and curious. You can pick up to three, so one label does not have to carry everything.

Being out in some places

If you are open in one part of your life and not another, the mutual-only option matters. Parts of your profile can be kept for mutual cruushes, so a stranger on the map sees less than someone you have cruushed.

Photos

Up to four, with no face required.

Saying it without explaining it

Your label is enough. You do not owe anyone an explanation of your history or what you have done before.

Meeting people of any gender

The map shows people of every gender. Use the labels on profiles to find the people you are interested in, and ignore anything else.
