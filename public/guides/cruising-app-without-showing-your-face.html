Using a cruising app without showing your face

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Is it allowed?

Yes. cruush is an adult app and does not require your face in a photo. Plenty of people post a torso, a tattoo, a silhouette or something else that is theirs without being identifiable.

How many photos can you have?

Up to four on your profile.

Who can see your photos?

Only signed-in people you have allowed. Photos are encrypted where they are stored, each with its own key, and they are only decrypted for a viewer who is allowed to see them.

What happens to your photos if you block someone?

Their access to your photos stops straight away, even for photos they already loaded.

Are photos checked?

Photos are checked automatically before they are published, and some go to a human reviewer. Anything that sexualizes minors is removed and reported, with no exceptions.

What do people post instead of a face?

Common choices are a torso, a tattoo, a silhouette, a hand, a pair of shoes, or something that shows personality without showing identity. Pick whatever you are comfortable having on a profile.

What will other people think?

Some people will skip a profile without a face, and some will not. If you are looking for people who respect your choice, say so in your profile.
