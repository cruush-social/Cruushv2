Cruising terms and what they mean

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Words about meeting

  • Cruising: looking for someone to meet, usually for sex or a casual connection, usually soon.
  • Hookup: a casual sexual meeting, often arranged the same day.
  • Discreet: keeping it private, whether because of work, family or just preference.
  • DL (down low): not out, and keeping any queer sex or relationships hidden.
  • Host: someone who has a place to meet. Traveling means going to the other person.

Words about people

  • Masc and femme: how someone presents. People use them for themselves, and they do not always match gender.
  • Butch and stud: terms many lesbian and queer women use for a masculine presentation.
  • Top, bottom and vers: roles in sex. Vers means versatile, comfortable with either.
  • Twink, bear, otter and cub: body type terms common among gay and bi men.

Words about how people behave

  • Ghosting: someone stops replying without saying why.
  • Catfishing: a profile that uses someone else's photos or a made-up identity.
  • Flaking: agreeing to meet and then not showing up.

Words about health

  • STI test: a screening for sexually transmitted infections.
  • PrEP: a daily or periodic medication that lowers the chance of getting HIV. A clinician can tell you whether it suits you.

Words on cruush

  • A cruush: a light way to say you are interested.
  • A mutual cruush: when the other person sends one back.
  • Ghost Mode: showing a decoy position on the map. It has nothing to do with ghosting.
  • Passing through: being on the map as someone who is not from here.
