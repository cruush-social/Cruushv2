Nonbinary cruising

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Many ways to say it

The gender list on cruush includes nonbinary, genderqueer, genderfluid, agender, gender nonconforming, demi-nonbinary, neutrois, pangender, polygender, third gender, two-spirit and more. You can choose several.

Presentation

Separate from gender, you can pick presentation labels such as androgynous, fluid, nonconforming or masc.

Pronouns

The list includes they/them, she/her, he/him, xe/xem, ze/zir and fae/faer, and an option that says to ask.

Photos

Up to four, none of them needs to show your face.

Describing yourself briefly

A few labels beat a paragraph. Pick the ones that you want people to see first, and leave the rest. You can change them whenever you like.

Meeting someone

If someone gets your pronouns wrong, you can correct them once, and you can block them if it continues. A cruising app does not require you to educate anyone.
