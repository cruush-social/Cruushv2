Where to meet discreetly

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Pick somewhere away from your own circles

A coffee shop in a neighborhood where you do not know anyone is easier than one near your office or your home. A different part of town, or the next town over, can help.

Places that tend to work

  • A quiet cafe or bar at an off-peak time.
  • A park in daylight.
  • A museum or a bookshop.
  • A hotel lobby or bar, if both of you are comfortable with it.

If you go somewhere private

  • Decide beforehand where you will go and who pays.
  • Choose a place with a front desk or other people nearby, especially the first time.
  • Tell a friend where you are, even if you cannot say who you are with.
  • Know how you are getting home.

Leave less of a trail

  • Pay in a way you are comfortable with, and think about what shows up on a card statement.
  • Turn off location sharing with people who do not need to know where you are.
  • Do not post photos or check in.

Have an exit

Agree that either of you can leave without an explanation. A discreet meeting works best when both people feel free to end it.
