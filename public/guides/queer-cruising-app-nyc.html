A queer cruising app for New York City

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Why is cruush a map first?

You open cruush and see a map. It shows who is nearby, who is passing through and who is up for meeting now. That is the whole idea. The rest of the app is there to make the map worth opening.

How do you say yes?

If you are interested in someone, you send them a cruush. Receiving one does not obligate you to anything. If they send one back, it is mutual, and more of each other's profile and location can open up.

What is cruush not?

It is not a feed of profiles to swipe, and it does not pretend to be a dating app. It is for meeting people near you, usually soon.

Who makes cruush?

cruush is developed by technosovereign, a creative technology company. It is independent and is not owned by a larger dating company. It runs on European infrastructure.

What are the three kinds of people on the map?

The map separates people who are nearby, people who are passing through, and people who are up for meeting now. If you are visiting for a night, it helps people understand what you are after. If you live here, it helps you see who might want something spontaneous.

How does cruush keep you private?

cruush does not show your exact position by default. You decide whether anyone sees it, and you can keep it for people you are mutual cruushes with. If you want to blur things further, Ghost Mode shows a decoy.

Can you move your pin?

You can move your pin to a place you are going, such as another borough or a different part of the city, so you can see who is around before you leave.

Where does cruush work?

It works in and around New York City, within about 100 miles of the city.
