Queer cruising app on the Connecticut shoreline

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

What should you know about the Connecticut shoreline?

Part of Connecticut is within about 100 miles of New York City, which covers the cities along Long Island Sound: Stamford, Norwalk, Bridgeport and New Haven. New Haven is home to Yale University.

How do you get around the Connecticut shoreline?

Metro-North's New Haven Line runs from Grand Central through Fairfield County to New Haven. Shore Line East and Amtrak serve parts of the coast farther along.

How do you use cruush in the Connecticut shoreline?

If you are traveling for the day, you can move your pin to your destination before you leave and see who is around.

Which areas are worth knowing in the Connecticut shoreline?

Stamford and Norwalk are closest to New York, in Fairfield County. Bridgeport is next, and New Haven is farther east.

How do you plan a night out in the Connecticut shoreline?

Metro-North's New Haven Line runs late but less often at night. Check the last train back to the city before you go.
