Your first time with a trans woman

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Is a trans woman a woman?

A trans woman is a woman. If you are attracted to her, you are attracted to a woman, and a first time with her is a first time with a woman. Treat her that way before, during and after.

How should you start?

Use her name and her pronouns. If you are not sure what words she uses for her body, ask her, in private, and use her words. Do not guess, and do not use words she has told you she dislikes.

What should you not assume?

  • Do not assume what she wants to do or what she does not want to do.
  • Do not assume what her body is like, or that it matches a picture in your head.
  • Ask what she likes, and let her tell you at her own pace.

Which questions should you skip?

Do not ask about surgery, hormones, medical history or her old name. Those are personal, and she will tell you if she wants you to know.

Why should you not fetishize her?

Do not describe her as an experiment, a fantasy or a category. Do not say anything that treats her as a type. She is a person you are meeting.

What about safety and discretion?

Some trans women have been harassed or threatened, so she may be careful about where you meet and who knows. Respect her choices, meet somewhere public first if she prefers, and never share her photos or her identity.

How do you look after your health?

Infection risk depends on what you do, not on anyone's gender. Condoms, gloves and dental dams lower the risk, and a clinician can advise you on testing and prevention.

What happens afterward?

Check in and thank her. Keep it private unless she says otherwise.
