Meet lesbians and queer women in NYC

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Who is on the map?

cruush is a location-based cruising app for queer people. It is not women-only, so you will see a mix of people. What you control is how you describe yourself and who gets close to you.

What should you do before you go out?

Say you are heading to Brooklyn or Queens tonight. Before you leave, you can move your pin to that neighborhood and look at who is around. If someone catches your eye, send a cruush. If they send one back, it is mutual, and that is when it makes sense to start talking about plans.

Which labels fit?

The sexuality list includes lesbian, sapphic, bisexual, pansexual and queer, and you can choose up to three. The gender list includes woman, cis woman, trans woman, femme and nonbinary. If you want to say more about how you present, there are options like butch, femme, stud and soft butch.

What can you keep back?

Parts of your profile, and your precise location, can be kept for mutual cruushes only. People who are not mutual with you see less.

How do you move around the city?

New York is five boroughs, and the subway runs all night, so most of the city is within an hour of wherever you are. That makes the useful question where you will be tonight, not where you live. Moving your pin lets the map answer it for you.

How do you say what you are looking for?

Some people on a cruising app want a hookup tonight, some want a date, and some want to meet new friends first. It helps everyone if you say which one you are, in your first message or on your profile. People who are looking for something different will move on, and that is the point.

How should the first meeting go?

Pick a public place, tell a friend where you are, and give yourself an easy way to leave. A cruising app is not a promise that anyone owes you more than a conversation.

Where does cruush work?

cruush works in and around New York City, within about 100 miles of it.
