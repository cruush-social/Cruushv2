What is a mutual cruush?

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

What is a cruush?

A cruush is a light way to say you are interested. It is not a message, so the other person has nothing to answer.

What makes a cruush mutual?

When the other person sends one back, it becomes mutual. Both of you chose it. That is the point of it.

What changes when a cruush is mutual?

Parts of your profile can be set to show only to mutual cruushes. You can also choose to share your precise location with them. Becoming mutual does not give anything away automatically. You decide what opens, and you can change your mind.

Is a mutual cruush private?

Who you are into is personal. cruush encrypts the record of a mutual cruush, along with the details around it.

What if you change your mind?

You can change what you share, or block the person at any time.

Does a mutual cruush mean you owe a reply?

Mutual means you both said you were interested. It does not mean you owe anyone a reply, a meeting, or anything else.
