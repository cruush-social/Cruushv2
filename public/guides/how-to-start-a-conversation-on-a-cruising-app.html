How to start a conversation on a cruising app

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Why the first message matters

People on a cruising app see a lot of messages. One that shows you read their profile and says what you want stands out, and one that just says hey usually gets lost.

What works

  • Mention one specific thing from their profile.
  • Say what you are looking for in a sentence.
  • Ask one question that is easy to answer.
  • Keep it short enough to read in a few seconds.

Examples

Something like: I like your photo of the bike. I am around Bushwick tonight and looking to meet someone. Are you free later?

Or when you are less sure: Your profile made me smile. Are you around this evening?

What to skip

  • Opening with a photo of your body that nobody asked for.
  • Long messages about yourself.
  • Asking for photos in the first message.
  • Sending the same text to everyone, because people can tell.

If there is no reply

Let it go. Sending one follow-up after a day is fine. After that, assume the answer is no and move on.
