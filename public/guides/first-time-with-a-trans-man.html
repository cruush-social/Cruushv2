Your first time with a trans man

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Is a trans man a man?

A trans man is a man. Use his name and his pronouns the whole way through, including in bed. Getting them wrong, even by accident, can undo a lot of trust.

What words should you use for his body?

If you are unsure what words he uses for his body, ask him privately, and use the words he gives you. Do not guess and do not use words he has said he dislikes.

What should you not assume?

  • Do not assume what he likes or what he does not.
  • Do not assume what his body is like.
  • Ask before you touch, and follow what he is comfortable with.

What if he binds?

Some trans men bind their chests. If he does, do not ask him to take it off or loosen it. Let him decide. Do not ask about surgery, hormones or medical history, and never ask about his old name.

Why should you not fetishize him?

He is not a curiosity or a way to explore something. Treat him as you would anyone you are attracted to.

How do you look after your health?

Infection risk depends on what you do, not on anyone's gender. Condoms, gloves and dental dams lower the risk, and a clinician can advise you on testing and prevention.

What happens afterward?

Check in and thank him. Keep it private unless he says otherwise.
