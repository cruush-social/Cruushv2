A cruising app for trans women

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Can I describe myself the way I want?

Yes. The gender list on cruush includes trans woman and transfemme, along with woman, femme, nonbinary and many other options, and you can pick more than one. You can change it later.

Do I have to show my face?

No. cruush does not require your face in a photo. You can have up to four photos, and only signed-in people you have allowed can see them.

Can people tell exactly where I am?

Not by default. They see an area, not a point. If you turn on Ghost Mode they see a decoy position instead. The decoy is built so that watching it over time does not lead back to where you actually are.

What if someone messages me and I do not want them to?

Block them. It works immediately and in both directions. Reporting is next to it, and a person reads every report.

Where does it work?

In and around New York City, within about 100 miles of it.

Taking it at your pace

You can look at the map without doing anything. A cruush is only interest, and you can ignore any you receive. Nothing happens unless you decide it should.

When you meet

Choose the place, and make it a public one the first time. Tell a friend where you are going. If anything feels off, leave. You do not owe anyone an explanation.
