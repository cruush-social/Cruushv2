Queer cruising app in Newark

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

What should you know about Newark?

Newark is the largest city in New Jersey and the seat of Essex County. Neighborhoods include the Ironbound, known for its Portuguese, Spanish and Brazilian restaurants, and the University Heights area near Rutgers-Newark and NJIT.

How do you get around Newark?

Newark Penn Station has PATH, NJ Transit and Amtrak service, and trains to Manhattan take less than half an hour. Newark Liberty International Airport is south of downtown.

How do you use cruush in Newark?

If you are arriving at the airport or the train station and want to see who is around before you get into the city, move your pin to Newark first.

Which areas are worth knowing in Newark?

Downtown is around Newark Penn Station and Military Park. The Ironbound is east of the station. University Heights is northwest of downtown.

How do you plan a night out in Newark?

PATH and NJ Transit run late but less often. If you are heading back to Manhattan, check the schedule before you leave.
