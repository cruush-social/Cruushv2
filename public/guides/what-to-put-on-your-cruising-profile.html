What to put on your cruising profile

Written by Julien Lenaz, lead developer of cruush. October 6, 2026

Photos

You can have up to four. You do not need to show your face. Pick ones that say something true about you.

Labels

Pick gender and sexuality labels that fit you. You can choose up to three sexuality labels, and you can add presentation labels and pronouns. All of it is optional.

Keep some back

Parts of your profile can be set to show only to mutual cruushes. Use that for details you do not want every stranger to see.

Leave out

  • Your home address.
  • Your workplace.
  • Your full name.

A short example of a good profile

Two or three photos that look like you, a few labels, a line about what you are looking for, and nothing that identifies where you live or work. That is enough.

Updating it

You can change your profile any time. If something is not getting the response you want, change a photo or a label and see what happens.
